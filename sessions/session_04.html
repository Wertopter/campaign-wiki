Synopsis

The trio assaulted Izanar's Spire and pushed through its dungeon to confront The Druid behind the attacks on Grethenwald. After a brutal fight, Zereth landed the killing blow. Searching the Spire afterward they recovered the Seed, a dark orb teeming with power. Each character to lay hands on it was shown a vision them wielding some sort ofcorrupted power. Zereth tucked the relic into his bag for safekeeping.
