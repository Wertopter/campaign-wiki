Synopsis

The party arrived at Luke's Keep. Clanker-san slipped down to the keep's dungeons and ate what he had taken for grain — in fact the cremated ashes of two individuals. Each ash gave him a memory: a man performing a human sacrifice to open a portal of fire and summon a demonic figure, and a woman abducting a drunk girl from a Greenport bar. Croak Monsieur tried to investigate the keep's second floor but was unable to gain access.
