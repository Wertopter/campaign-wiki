Synopsis

The party first crossed paths at the southern gate of Grethenwald as it was attacked by a corrupted bear, two groups of minor treants, and a young dryad. After the assault was driven off, Captain Ellis Rothar of the town guard hired the heroes to investigate the corruption and the matching disappearances of merchants along the western road. The night ended at The Lucky Dog tavern, where they made a friend in its keeper, Cane Dolgurr.
