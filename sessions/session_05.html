Synopsis

Clanker-san and Croak Monsieur reunited with the rest of the party at Izanar's Spire just as the others were exploring its peak. Together they used a key to open Izanar Leafwood's hidden tomb, fought through its guardians, and looted it. Returning to Grethenwald, the party collected their reward from Captain Rothar. Glen Zuckuss pulled Zereth aside in private with a message from The Glupp Shittos and Yaddle: they were pleased with his progress, and his next task was to attend the meeting of the Free Cities in Greenport. Captain Rothar also passed Clanker-san fresh information on Rathius Malabel. The session marked the end of Act 1, and the party leveled up to 2.
