Synopsis

The party took two weeks of downtime in Grethenwald to prepare for the journey to Greenport. During that stretch each of them was visited by a vivid dream:

  • Obrog walked endless library halls lined with tomes; looking down he saw his body had become bare bones still wearing his armor.
  • Zereth first saw his brother urging him to defy the Council, then woke in a cave of chained non-Faerie miners pulling Syrenite, where exposure to a powerful crystal sent surging power through his wings.
  • Clanker-san drempt as a Fungril child that, running and playing in The Fungal Isles before being snared and abducted by armored figures.
  • Croak Monsieur fled a consuming black fog, filling the alleys of Raften, and was shot through the hand by a red arrow when jumping over a wall. He woke with the mark of an open red eye on the back of his hand.

Captain Rothar formally asked Zereth to represent Grethenwald at the upcoming meeting in Greenport, mentioned that the Grand Archives there might hold answers for Obrog and Scalestaff, and reported that Rathius Malabel and his group — including a Grogian Monsieur who shared Croak's surname — had been seen passing east through the gates toward Vaes Ithra.
