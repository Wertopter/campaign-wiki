Synopsis

At dinner in Luke's Keep, Willem Kaavlir revealed himself as master of the keep and offered the party its deed if they descended into the dungeons and slew "The Beast." Zereth slipped away during the meal to investigate the upper floors and discovered the symbol of the Lord of Light on the table of Kaavlir's study — marking their host as something far more dangerous than a generous lord. The session ended with the party split and in trouble: Clanker-san and Croak Monsieur left missing, Obrog and Scalestaff still seated at the dinner table, and Zereth alone with what he had just learned.
