Synopsis

Following the western road out of Grethenwald, the party found a wrecked merchant caravan splashed with blood — with only food taken — and bloody tracks leading off into the forest. They picked up the trail and followed it to the cave of Ephel Mork. While trying to clear the corrupted roots along a pathway through the cave, Zereth set a fire that woke the great dryad slumbering inside. They were also formally introduced to Glen Zuckuss, the Captain's halfling assistant, before setting out.
