Religion

Ael'thuun, the Dawnbringer

Greater God · Light, Renewal

Patron of paladins and farmers alike. Said to have shattered her own heart into shards to seal away the Drowned God during the Cataclysm.

The Drowned God

Fallen God · Sea, Ruin

Bound beneath the waves a thousand years ago. His cult grows bolder with each passing year.

Major Historical Events

The Cataclysm (1,000 years ago)

A war among the gods that drowned an entire continent. Most surviving knowledge of the old world was lost.

The Long Silence (300 years ago)

Three centuries during which the gods did not answer prayers. Ended abruptly, and nobody agrees on why.

The Founding of Highspire (180 years ago)

Refugees from the broken kingdoms gathered around the white cliffs and raised the walls of what would become the capital of Veshmar.

Ancestries

Humans

Widespread · Adaptable

The most numerous ancestry across the kingdoms. Found in every city, every trade, and every faction — for better or worse.

Dwarves

Mountain holds · Clan-bound

Master smiths and stoneworkers of the Ironspine peaks. Many clans were scattered when the frost giants descended a generation ago.

Elves

Whisperwood & the Far Shore · Long-lived

Divided between the fey-touched wood elves of the Whisperwood and the seafaring high elves of the Far Shore enclaves.

Halflings

River villages · Riverfolk

Most halflings live along the great rivers and coasts, working as traders, sailors, and (occasionally) less reputable professions.

Half-Orcs

Borderlands · Mixed heritage

Common in the frontier holds where human and orc communities meet. Often serve as mercenaries, scouts, or village protectors.

Tieflings

Rare · Touched by old pacts

Born to families whose ancestors made bargains during the Long Silence. Distrusted in the heartlands, welcomed in Highspire.
