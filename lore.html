Religion

The Old Gods

Lower Planes · Chatoic · Sealed

The Old Gods were those of evil. Another name for the Old Gods would be the Lower Gods as they now occupy The Lower Plane. These gods used to rule over Ai' Taleth, but were banished to the The Lower Planes by The New Gods 2800 years ago during The Great Betrayal when Felnor, The Betrayer, betrayed the Old Gods and helped The New Gods send them throughout The Lower Planes.

The New Gods

Upper Planes · Orderly · Most accepted

The New Gods are the deities of The Higher Planes and have the largest religious following in Ai' Taleth. They represent good and order as they influence The Material Plane from above.

The Lord of Light

Monotheism · Anti-Magic · Extreme · Young

The Lord of Light is the monotheistic god followed by the Gaimond Kingdom. Those who follow him are known for their extreme anti-magic beliefs and imperialistic views. By his followers, he is believed to be the being that saved the world from the Devastation.

Major Historical Events

The Devestation (800 years ago)

Arcane ritual went wrong. Asmodeus, The King of Hell and Sapila, the Goddess of Rot, were both successfully summoned to the Material Plane, resulting in the destruction of the Holy Valir Empire and the creation of the Infernis and the Fungril.

PLACEHOLDER The Long Silence (300 years ago)

Three centuries during which the gods did not answer prayers. Ended abruptly, and nobody agrees on why.

PLACEHOLDER The Founding of Highspire (180 years ago)

Refugees from the broken kingdoms gathered around the white cliffs and raised the walls of what would become the capital of Veshmar.

Ancestries

Humans

Widespread · Adaptable

The most numerous ancestry across the kingdoms. Found in every city, every trade, and every faction — for better or worse.

Dwarves

Mountain holds · Clan-bound

Master smiths and stoneworkers of the Ironspine peaks. Many clans were scattered when the frost giants descended a generation ago.

Elves

Whisperwood & the Far Shore · Long-lived

Divided between the fey-touched wood elves of the Whisperwood and the seafaring high elves of the Far Shore enclaves.

Halflings

River villages · Riverfolk

Most halflings live along the great rivers and coasts, working as traders, sailors, and (occasionally) less reputable professions.

Half-Orcs

Borderlands · Mixed heritage

Common in the frontier holds where human and orc communities meet. Often serve as mercenaries, scouts, or village protectors.

Tieflings

Rare · Touched by old pacts

Born to families whose ancestors made bargains during the Long Silence. Distrusted in the heartlands, welcomed in Highspire.
