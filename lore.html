The Pantheon

Ael'thuun, the Dawnbringer

Greater God · Light, Renewal

Patron of paladins and farmers alike. Said to have shattered her own heart into shards to seal away the Drowned God during the Cataclysm.

The Drowned God

Fallen God · Sea, Ruin

Bound beneath the waves a thousand years ago. His cult grows bolder with each passing year.

Factions

The Spire of Whispers

An order of arcane scholars headquartered in Highspire. Patrons of the party, though their motives are not always clear.

The Crimson Hand

A ruthless mercenary company for hire. Currently working for someone who wants the party dead.

Historical Events

The Cataclysm (1,000 years ago)

A war among the gods that drowned an entire continent. Most surviving knowledge of the old world was lost.

The Long Silence (300 years ago)

Three centuries during which the gods did not answer prayers. Ended abruptly, and nobody agrees on why.
