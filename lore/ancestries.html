A reference to the major ancestries of the campaign world. For broader context on religion and history, see Lore & History.

Humans

Overview

Widespread · Adaptable · Short-lived

The most numerous ancestry across the kingdoms. Humans dominate the trade routes, the standing armies, and most thrones. Their adaptability is matched only by their ambition.

Homelands

The Kingdom of Veshmar, the Free Cities of the Saltmere coast, and countless village-states between.

Dwarves

Overview

Mountain holds · Clan-bound · Long-lived

Master smiths and stoneworkers of the Ironspine peaks. Dwarven life revolves around clan, craft, and the deep memory of grievances both ancient and recent.

The Scattering

A generation ago, frost giants descended from the high passes and shattered three of the great clanholds. Many dwarves now live in exile among human cities, awaiting the day of reclamation.

Elves

Overview

Whisperwood & Far Shore · Very long-lived

Divided between two great branches that rarely agree on anything, including whether they should be considered the same people.

Wood Elves of the Whisperwood

Bound by old pacts to the Court of Thorns. Time moves strangely where they dwell, and outsiders rarely leave with the same memories they brought in.

High Elves of the Far Shore

Seafaring scholars and merchant-princes. Their floating enclaves drift between the western isles trading lore for goods.

Halflings

Overview

River villages & ports · Riverfolk

Most halflings live along the great rivers and coasts, working as traders, sailors, dockhands, and — when opportunity calls — far less reputable professions.

The Quickfoot Compact

A loose network of halfling families that trades information as freely as cargo. If something moved on a river in the last decade, a Quickfoot knows about it.

Half-Orcs

Overview

Borderlands · Mixed heritage

Common in the frontier holds where human and orc communities meet. Often serve as mercenaries, scouts, or village protectors — roles that demand both strength and the trust of two peoples.

Tieflings

Overview

Rare · Touched by old pacts

Born to families whose ancestors made bargains during the Long Silence, when the gods would not answer. Distrusted in the heartlands, welcomed openly in Highspire, and quietly recruited by the Spire of Whispers.
