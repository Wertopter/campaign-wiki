Ancestries of Ai'Taleth

Many ancestries exist across Ai'Taleth. Some are bound to a single continent; others are found the world over. They fall into three broad categories: Common, Exotic, and Shunned.

Common

Clank

Made often of metal, wood, stone or other materials, Clanks are Humanoid machines built to serve a purpose.

Dwarf

Mountain-dwellers of Thul. Many dwarven cities are carved into the mountains themselves, though dwarves also live alongside humans, elves, giantborne, and halflings in the larger cities.

Elf

Long-lived and reclusive, mostly keeping to the remote corners of Thul. Split into three branches: Wood Elves (forest hunters and nature-mages), High Elves (intellectuals and arcane scholars), and Sand Elves (a desert people who use little magic beyond wind-craft and survive through specialized technology). The oldest elves still remember the Devastation.

Giantborne

Descendants of the giants who came to Thul from the giant continent. Originally a savage force along the coasts, many eventually settled — some into mountain tribes, others as farmers and laborers in human cities.

Halfling

A whimsical, magical people of the Great Plains of Thul. Before humans arrived, they lived in small camps and warred with the goblins of the plains until the Holy Valir Empire aided them in the Thousand Year War.

Human

Originally native only to Valiris. About 1,500 years ago Dorar Marcus crossed to Thul, founded the first settlements, and triggered the great migration that brought humanity to the main continent.

Exotic

Dracona

Said to be descendants of dragons, made by Bahamut in the image of humans. Red, black, and white Dracona hail from the volcanic Gellmir Mountains on Thul; green and blue Dracona are also found on Khard.

Faun

Originally from the Feywild but long disconnected from their fey ancestors. They occupy the Weirwood from the base of the Gellmir Mountains to the Great Plains, rarely leaving the forest except to trade. Known for moonlit dancing, fine wine, and guarding the woods.

Firbolg

Nature-attuned, bovine humanoids rooted in fey magic. They see themselves as shepherds of the natural world and its ties to the Feywild.

Galapa

No notes yet.

Katari

Explorers of the natural world who keep largely to themselves. Rarely seen, but known to travel throughout the continent of Thul.

Ribbet

Energetic, frog-like people with bright personalities and fearless enthusiasm. They favor humid, boggy regions and are common in northern Thul and on the island of Raften.

Simiah

Native to Raften, though some claim origin on the continent Zerg. Those from Zerg appear far more technologically advanced than their kin on Raften.

Shunned

Faerie

Insect-like humanoids that are known to be rare and mysterious. They are often seen as a bad omen and are often avoided by other races.

Fungril

A people born during the Devastation in 0 A.D. with the emergence of Sapila, the Goddess of Rot. They are made up of those that used to inhabit the Blessed Isles (known as the Fungal Isles).

Goblin

Once numerous on the Great Plains and Barbaria, where they terrorized halflings and tinkered with new inventions. After the Thousand Year War, humans and halflings drove most goblins out of the Great Plains and into Barbaria.

Infernis

A young people born during the Devastation in 0 A.D., descended from the fiends that overran Valiris after the fall of the Holy Valir Empire. Their fiendish heritage has marked them as a shunned people across the world.

Orc

A resilient, warlike people common on the plains of Barbaria, where most live in small tribal bands while the strongest rule as tyrant warlords from castles. Some have left Barbaria for the Gaimond Kingdom, the Free Cities, or the Frigid Reach of Faelán.
