Cities & Settlements

Saltmere

Coastal port city · Population ~12,000

A bustling harbor town where the party first met. Famous for its fish markets, smuggling, and the Lighthouse of Vaelin.

Highspire

Capital of the Kingdom of Veshmar

A walled city of white stone built around the great library and the royal palace. Seat of Queen Aerith II.

Dungeons & Ruins

The Sunken Vault

Submerged temple · Cult stronghold

A flooded pre-Cataclysm temple now occupied by cultists of the Drowned God. Said to contain the Shard of Ael'thuun.

The Hollow Crown

Ancient barrow · Cleared in Session 7

A burial mound of the forgotten Kings of Bone. The party recovered a silvered crown and disturbed something that still hunts them.

Regions

The Whisperwood

Ancient forest · Fey-touched

Time moves strangely beneath these silver-barked trees. Home to the Court of Thorns and at least one very polite dryad.
