Continents

Thul

Largest · Where the story takes place

The largset continent in Ai' Taleth and the home to many different cultures and civilizations.

Valiris

Destroyed · Ancient · Perilous

The destroyed continent of Ai' Taleth that is now a dangerous and unforgiving place as it has been overrun by demons, devils, and rot. None who venture into Valiris are never seen again.

Skyrr

Giant · Unexplored

Skyrr is the home of the ancient giants who once occupied ancient Thul. No living humanoid has ever set foot on Skyrr. Some believe it only exists in legend and myth.
