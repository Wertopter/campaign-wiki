Prominent Civilizations

The Free Cities

Northeastern Thul · City-States

A collection of city-states in the northeastern part of Thul. Known for their trade, independence, and sovereignty from the other city-states of the region.

The Gaimond Kingdom

Eastern Thul · Kingdom · Extreme Religious Beliefs

An ancient kingdom who's royal family dates back to the Holy Valir Empire. Known for it's extreme religious beliefs and it's military strength. Follows the Lord of Light as it's patron god.

Faelán

The Frigid Reach · Magocracy

A magocracy in the frozen south of Thul, ruled by the Council of Colors — the five strongest mages in the realm, each representing one of the colors of arcane power.

Arènal

The Weirwood · Sun Elf Kingdom

A secluded kingdom of sun elves deep within the Weirwood. Little trade or diplomacy reaches outsiders; the realm keeps its affairs hidden beneath the forest canopy.

The Dunsman Collective

The Gellmir Mountains · Confederation

A confederation of kingdoms in the Gellmir Mountains, home to dwarves, giantborne, and dracona who share the high peaks and forge uneasy alliances against the harsh interior of the range.

The Sindari Elves

The Erguld Forest · Moon Elves

A rarely seen people of moon elves who dwell in the Erguld Forest. Travelers speak of glimpses among the western trees, but the Sindari seldom reveal themselves or their purpose to outsiders.
