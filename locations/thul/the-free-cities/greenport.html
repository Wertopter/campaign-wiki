Overview

Greenport is a major city of the Free Cities in northeastern Thul, with an estimated population of around 500,000. It was the first place the Holy Valir Empire landed when they came to Thul. The city is governed by Governor Elrick Winshaw, a plump halfling man — a merchant turned politician.

The Grand Archives

Some claim it to be the largest library on Thul, holding much ancient knowledge from previous epochs.

Four Gardens

Home to one of the largest trade markets in the known world, and among the greatest commercial districts in the Free Cities.
